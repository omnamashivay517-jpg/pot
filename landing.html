Our Story

Hot Cup started with a simple realization: India grows some of the world's finest beans, but the true artisanal experience was often reserved for export markets. In 2018, we set out to change that.

We traveled across the Western Ghats, partnering directly with third-generation farmers who treat coffee plants like family members. Today, our roastery stands as a bridge between tradition and contemporary technique.
